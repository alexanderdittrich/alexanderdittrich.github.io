About

I am a doctoral researcher in the Laboratory of Intelligent Systems at EPFL, supervised by Prof. Dario Floreano. My research centres on tensegrity robots: lightweight, compliant machines in which rigid struts are held together by a web of cables.

My work ranges from designing novel tensegrity robots and understanding how they behave to exploring learning and adaptation mechanisms that control them.

Before EPFL, I studied Mechatronics at the University of Stuttgart and gained experience at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, Fraunhofer IPA, Robert Bosch, and in Formula Student.
